Operating rules
01

Two active items, maximum. One for work, one for you. The Active gauge turns cherry past two. That number climbing is the failure mode, not slow progress.

02

Slip the whole chain, don't compress it. If Network+ starts three weeks late, everything after it moves three weeks. Squeezing a module to protect a date is how two things get done badly instead of one done well.

03

Finish before you start. Oweless is the test. It's nearly done and unshipped, and it gates the certification deliberately — if a project that's 90% finished can't be closed, an eight-week study plan won't go better.

04

Anything you add lands in a module, not on a pile. New work has to say which phase it belongs to. That's the whole defence against the list quietly growing back to eight parallel things.
