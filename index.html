Operating rules
01

Two active items, maximum. One for work, one for you. That count climbing is the failure mode, not slow progress.

02

Slip the chain, don't compress it. If one module starts three weeks late, everything after moves three weeks. Squeezing a module to protect a date is how two things get done badly instead of one done well.

03

Finish before you start. Oweless is the test. It's nearly done and unshipped, and it gates the certification deliberately.

04

Anything you add lands in a module. New work has to say which phase it belongs to. That's the defence against the list quietly growing back.
